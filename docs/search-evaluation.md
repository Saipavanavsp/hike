## What each feature does

- **Typo correction** (`services/query_understanding.py`): a word that matches no open job is replaced by the closest title/skill word (PostgreSQL `pg_trgm` similarity and `fuzzystrmatch` edit distance, same first letter, at most 1-2 edits). The UI says "Showing results for ...".
- **Synonyms** (`app/ml/data/search_synonyms.json`): "js" also matches JavaScript, "k8s" Kubernetes, "sde" software engineer, and so on.
- **Hybrid ranking**: text relevance = (1 - w) x lexical + w x semantic, where semantic is the MiniLM cosine between the query and the job (rescaled). Jobs that share no words with the query are still candidates when the cosine is at least the threshold.

## Limitations

- 37 queries over 50 jobs is small; group means have 8-11 queries each.
- Queries and labels were written by the project author with AI assistance.
- Recency is part of the production ranking and depends on when the data was seeded.
